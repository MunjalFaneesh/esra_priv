This is the early prototype test stand used to bring up a single leg. It is
documented here so it can be rebuilt from the parts list alone.

<Callout kind="note" title="Status">
  This is a prototype, not a finished design. Several lines in the BOM are still
  outstanding, and the timing-belt drive has not been fully specified.
</Callout>

## Stand

The stand is built around two 800&nbsp;mm SBR20 linear rails with four SBR20UU
blocks, which gives a vertical axis long enough to let the leg travel through a
full stride without hitting a limit.

## Actuators

Three quasi-direct-drive actuators drive the leg. Each is built around an 8110
stator, hand-wound, running on a Flipsky ODESC V4.2 controller in field-oriented
control.

<MarginNote n="1">
  Magnet count is 42 per rotor, 126 across the three actuators. The BOM records
  this as `42 x 3 = 126` — the expression is kept rather than flattened, because
  it carries the per-unit reasoning.
</MarginNote>

Rotors, planetary gears, the planet holder, stator lock, outer housing and ring
gear, bearing retaining ring, and backplate are all printed. The magnet holders
are laser-cut from 1.2&nbsp;mm A36/1008 mild steel.<sup>1</sup>
