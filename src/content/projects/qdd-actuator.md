The actuator is built around an 8110 stator wound by hand with 26 AWG
enamelled copper, with a printed rotor carrying 42 N52 magnets and a printed
planetary reduction. It runs on a Flipsky ODESC V4.2 in field-oriented
control.

The full parts list, including the test stand it was brought up on, is in the
[leg test stand guide](/docs/leg-test-stand).

Design credit for the actuator geometry goes to Nachum Twersky, whose
[published build](https://www.instructables.com/3D-Printed-QDD-Robotic-Actuator-MIT-Mini-Cheetah-C/)
this follows.
